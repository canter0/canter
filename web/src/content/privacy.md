# Privacy Policy

Last updated November 25, 2026.

This notice explains what Canter collects when you visit canter.dev or use the service, why we need it, and what choices you have.

## What we collect and why

- **Account and sign-in information.** We store your email address and account ID to identify your account, verify your email, and send sign-in, recovery, and security messages. If you sign in through Google or GitHub, we receive your email and an account identifier from that provider. If you set a password, we store a password hash. For passkeys, we store the credential ID and public key data needed to verify sign-ins. Your passkey’s private key stays with your authenticator or passkey provider. Canter doesn’t receive your fingerprint or face data.
- **Sessions and security information.** We use cookies for sign-in and related security checks. We may record IP addresses, browser information, and sign-in or security events to manage sessions, troubleshoot problems, and prevent abuse. For personal API keys, Canter stores a hash and metadata such as the key name, expiration date, and last use.
- **Workspace and workload data.** What we process depends on the features you use. It can include workspace membership, connected repository information and code, hosted files and databases, sandbox files and snapshots, CI logs and artifacts, conversations, attachments, and records of changes you or your agents make. We use this information to run your workloads, show results, and help you manage or troubleshoot your workspace. What other people can see depends on what you share, workspace permissions, and whether you make a workload or link public.
- **Billing and support.** Stripe processes card payments. Canter receives billing identifiers, invoice and payment status, and usage records so we can calculate charges, reconcile payments, and assist with billing. If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to send so we can respond.
- **Website measurement.** On the homepage and pricing page, we use a first-party cookie that lasts up to 30 days to count visits and understand how people find Canter. We record a broad referral category and the landing page. We may associate those with a signup. This measurement doesn’t collect full referrer URLs or search terms and is disabled when your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track.

## When information goes to others

We use service providers for hosting and storage, payment processing, account email, abuse prevention, and AI features. For example, we use Stripe for payments, Resend for account email, and Cloudflare Turnstile for bot checks when enabled. If you connect GitHub, a model provider, or another integration, we share the information needed for the feature you use with that provider.

When you use a hosted AI feature, the question or prompt and relevant context may be sent to OpenRouter and the model provider handling the request. For Ask Canter on the homepage, that includes your question and recent turns needed to answer a follow-up. Keep credentials and other sensitive information out of public FAQ questions.

Providers may process information outside your country, where local laws apply. We may disclose information when required by law or when legally permitted to address abuse, fraud, or threats to safety.

## Retention and your choices

We retain information as needed to provide the service, keep it secure, handle billing and disputes, and comply with legal obligations. You can manage sign-in methods and request account deletion in [account settings](/app/account). You may need to close or transfer active resources, billing, or shared work first. Shared workspace records can remain with that workspace, and some information may remain in backups or records we need to retain for legal, billing, or security purposes.

To ask about our handling of your information or request access, correction, or deletion, email [security@canter.dev](mailto:security@canter.dev). We may verify your identity before acting on a request. We’ll update this page when our practices change and provide notice of material changes as required by law. See also our [Terms of Service](/terms).
